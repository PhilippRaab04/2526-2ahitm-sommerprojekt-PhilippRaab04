Tutorial

Welcome to Quiz Dungeon. In this game you fight your way through the dungeon by answering quiz questions and defeating every boss on your path.

Movement:
Move your character with W, A, S, D. Walk into objects, answer fields and interaction boxes to interact with them.

Starting a Run:
Walk into Start Quiz Battle, enter your name and begin your run. After that, the first boss will appear.

Answering Questions:
Move onto the answer you think is correct and press E to lock in your choice.

Question Order:
Every boss gives you 2 easy, 2 medium and 2 hard questions. The questions are chosen randomly from the question pool.

Points:
Correct easy answers give +1 point, medium answers +2, and hard answers +3.

Hearts:
If you answer incorrectly, you lose hearts. If all hearts are gone, the run ends and the dungeon is failed.

Items:
During a question, walk into the Items box to open your inventory. There you can see all items you currently own and use them.

Item Effects:
50:50 Amulet removes two wrong answers.
Extra Life gives you one additional heart.
Question Switch replaces the current question with another one of the same difficulty.

Rewards:
Rewards can appear during your run. Walk into the Rewards box to claim them.

Your Goal:
Defeat all bosses, survive the dungeon and reach the end with as many points as possible.
